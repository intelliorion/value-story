# Bid Library Assistant — Status Report
Period: 2026-06
Prepared by: Head of Bid Management

Rollout complete across the Infrastructure Advisory bid team (14 coordinators)
as of 2026-04-30. Q2 is the first full quarter of operation.

Measured against the contract management export:

- Median days to first draft fell from 8.6 to 3.2.
- Short-notice tenders declined for capacity reasons fell from 8 to 2.
- Coordinator hours per response fell from 62 to 37.

Not yet measured:

- We have no baseline for response quality, so we cannot say whether win rate
  moved. The commercial team has asked us not to claim it.
- We have not attempted to count cost avoided. Nobody has agreed a rate.

New capability worth naming: the assistant surfaces precedent from practices
the coordinator has never worked in. Two Q2 water-sector responses reused
material from the rail team's 2019 submissions. That did not previously
happen at all — not slowly, but never.
