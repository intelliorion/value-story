# Bid Library Assistant — Project Brief
Northgate Consulting — Infrastructure Advisory
Owner: Head of Bid Management, Head of Bid Management
Date: 2026-02-10

## The problem

Northgate submits roughly 140 tender responses a year. Each response reuses
material from past submissions, but that library is 11 years of unsorted
documents across three legacy systems. Bid coordinators report spending the
first two days of every response hunting for precedent rather than writing.

Two consequences we hear repeatedly:

- Turnaround is dominated by search, not by drafting. Short-notice tenders
  (under 10 working days) are routinely declined because we cannot assemble
  a first draft in time.
- Quality is uneven. Whoever has been here longest writes the best response,
  because they remember where the good material is.

## What we are building

A retrieval assistant over the full submission library. A coordinator
describes the tender; the assistant returns the five most relevant past
sections with provenance, and drafts a first-pass response scaffold.

It does not submit anything. A bid coordinator edits and owns every word.
